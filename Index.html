    The motions, step by step

    Pitching

    Five moves, in this order.

    1. Set your stanceStand tall on the mound, feet shoulder width apart, ball hidden in your glove.
    2. Wind upLift your front knee while your hands stay together at your chest.
    3. StrideStep toward the batter and bring the ball back with your throwing arm.
    4. ReleaseWhip your arm forward and let go of the ball out in front of you.
    5. Follow throughLet your arm swing across your body and finish balanced, ready to field.

    Batting

    Five moves, in this order.

    1. Take your stanceStand beside the plate, knees slightly bent, bat held up near your back shoulder.
    2. LoadShift your weight to your back foot and keep your eyes on the pitcher.
    3. StrideTake a small step forward as the ball is thrown, keeping your hands back.
    4. SwingTurn your hips first, then your shoulders, and bring the bat through the ball.
    5. Follow throughFinish with the bat wrapped over your shoulder and your weight on your front foot.
